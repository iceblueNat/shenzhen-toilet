Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes. All 4,680 toilet locations and the metro map are bundled in the app. Only Apple Maps directions and Apple in-app purchases need a network connection.

Why does the app need my location?
Only to sort restrooms by distance and show “nearby”. If you deny permission, you can still browse the map manually.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no ads.

What do the tips do?
Tips are optional. They support data updates and maintenance. They do not unlock any feature — the app is fully usable for free.

How do I report a wrong toilet location?
Email us with the toilet name (Chinese name is helpful) and, if possible, a screenshot. We update the bundled data with each release.

Data sources

  • Metro restroom and interchange information: official Shenzhen Metro restroom directory.
  • Map display: Apple MapKit.
