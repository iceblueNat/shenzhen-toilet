Privacy Policy

Shenzhen Toilet · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Summary

Shenzhen Toilet does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Location is used only on your device to show nearby public toilets. Toilet data is bundled in the app and works offline.

Location data

With your permission, the app reads your device location while the app is in use. This is used solely to:

  • show your position on the map, and
  • sort toilets by distance and calculate walking time.

Location is processed on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, not stored by us, and not shared with third parties. If you deny location permission, the app remains usable through manual map browsing.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking across apps.
  • No usage history or search history sent to us.

Third-party services

  • Apple Maps / MapKit — map display and directions. Subject to Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Apple In-App Purchase — optional tips are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
